Standard-plane classification in intrapartum ultrasound

A ResNet-18 that scores a transperineal ultrasound frame for whether the pubic symphysis and fetal head are both resolved well enough to measure angle of progression. It runs entirely in your browser — the image never leaves your device.

ResNet-18 trained on the IUGC 2024 intrapartum ultrasound dataset (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.17655183, CC-BY-4.0). Inference runs locally via onnxruntime-web; no image is transmitted or stored. Method, results and code: github.com/AkhilTeja2209/intrapartum-plane-density.

Research artefact, not a diagnostic tool. Trained only on third-trimester transperineal video; it returns a confident number for any image, including ones nothing like its training data.
